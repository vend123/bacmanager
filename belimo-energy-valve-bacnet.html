Belimo Energy Valve
BACnet Points List & Setup

// before you start

Which Energy Valve is it?

🔍

Find the Ethernet socket

Belimo's own rule: if the Ethernet socket sits on the actuator, the valve is a generation 1, 2 or 3 with its own BACnet document. If it sits on the flow sensor, it is an Energy Valve 4 — this page. It covers the DN 15–50 models EV..R2+BAC, EV..R2+MID and EV..R3+BAC.

🌐

IP or MS/TP

The same valve does BACnet/IP (UDP 47808 from the factory, also as a foreign device with a BBMD) or BACnet MS/TP on RS-485. For MS/TP from a PC see BACnet MS/TP over RS-485.

📋

What the valve serves

Per Belimo's PICS: B-ASC, protocol revision 14, ReadPropertyMultiple, WriteProperty, COV (at most 6 subscriptions, up to 8 hours each), no segmentation, no alarms, schedules or trend logs. BTL-listed.

// setup

// control

Tell the setpoint to listen to BACnet

⚙

SpSource = Bus

The setpoint SpRel (AO1) does nothing while SpSource (MV122) is 1: Analog — the factory value, which takes the setpoint from 0.5–10 V on wire 3. Write 2: Bus once. With nothing on wire 3, a valve left on Analog just sits closed whatever the BMS writes.

%

What the % means

ControlMode (MV100) decides: 1 position, 2 flow (factory), 3 power, 4 differential pressure. In flow control SpRel is a share of the flow range up to V'max (AV94), in power control of P'max (AV110). Changing ControlMode resets the valve, so set it at commissioning, not from a BMS program.

⎘

Override

Override (MV1) is commandable: 1 None, 2 Open, 3 Close, 4 Minimum, 6 Maximum, 7 Nominal, 11 Motor stop. SpRel's default is 0 %, so a BMS that relinquishes its setpoint leaves the valve at 0.

// the rules that bite

A watchdog that wants to hear from you

120 s

Bus watchdog

If BusFailAction (MV130) is anything but 1: None, the valve expects SpRel (AO1) or Override (MV1) to be written within BusWatchdog (AV130) — 5 to 3600 s, 120 from the factory. If neither is, it goes to the fail position (open, close, max, min or stop) and sets bit 15 of ErrorState (AV140).

↻

Writing on change is not enough

Many BMS programs write a setpoint only when it changes. On a steady load that is no write at all, and after the watchdog time the valve drops to its fail position. The setpoint has to be written again, cyclically, faster than AV130.

⚠

…but not everything

Belimo marks every persistent writable object from instance 90 up — SpSource, ControlMode, the watchdog, the flow and power limits, the unit selectors — as not to be written on a regular basis. Only SpRel and Override are meant for cyclic writes.

In BACManager a BACnet tag has Refresh (s): the last value written to it is written again every that many seconds until it is relinquished. The free workspace sets 10 s on SpRel (AO1) and on nothing else. Tested against the simulated valve with bus fail action Close and a 30-second watchdog, SpRel written once at 60 %: with Refresh the valve held its position for the whole 90-second run; without it, ErrorState went to 32768 (bit 15) about 30 s after the write and the valve closed.

The Energy Valve workspace

One dashboard page for an Energy Valve 4 on a chilled-water AHU coil: flow and flow setpoint, position, both temperatures and delta T, cooling power and energy, control mode and error state — with SpRel written again every 10 seconds, Override, and the commissioning writes (SpSource, bus fail action, watchdog) kept apart.

1

Discover the valve

On the BACnet tab open BACnet/IP on the PC's network card (or MS/TP on its RS-485 port at the trunk's baud rate) and press Discover. The valve appears with its instance — 1 if nobody has changed it.

2

Open the workspace

Workspace → Open. The file reads device 1; if yours has another instance, BACManager asks which discovered device takes its place and moves every point over.

3

Run, then command once

BACManager starts reading the valve by itself. On the Dashboard press Run, set SpSource to Bus once, then the setpoint — from then on SpRel is written again every 10 seconds. After BACManager restarts, write it once more.

Belimo Energy Valve BACnet — FAQ

Where is the Belimo Energy Valve BACnet points list?+
In Belimo's BACnet Interface Description for the Energy Valve DN 15…50 (Version 4); generations 1 to 3 have a separate document. The table on this page lists the points a BMS uses most.
How do I enable BACnet on the Energy Valve 4?+
The protocol is None from the factory. Choose BACnet/IP or BACnet MS/TP with the Belimo Assistant app over NFC, or in the valve's Web View, and set the instance, port or MAC and baud rate before connecting it to the BACnet network.
What is the Energy Valve's default BACnet device instance?+
1. The MS/TP MAC is also 1 and the baud rate 38400; BACnet/IP uses UDP port 47808. Give every valve its own instance.
Why doesn't the Energy Valve follow the BACnet setpoint?+
SpRel (AO1) only acts when SpSource (MV122) is 2: Bus. From the factory it is 1: Analog, and the valve follows the 0.5–10 V signal on wire 3 instead.
What is the Energy Valve bus watchdog?+
With a bus fail action set in MV130, the valve moves to that position if neither SpRel (AO1) nor Override (MV1) is written within BusWatchdog (AV130), 120 s from the factory, and sets bit 15 in ErrorState (AV140). The BMS has to write the setpoint again cyclically.
Which Energy Valve points may a BMS write cyclically?+
SpRel (AO1) and Override (MV1). Belimo asks that persistent writable objects from instance 90 up — setpoint source, control mode, watchdog, limits and units — are not written on a regular basis.
