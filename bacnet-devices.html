// questions

BACnet device guides — FAQ

Where do I find a BACnet points list for my device?+
In the maker's BACnet manual, PICS or EDE list. Each guide here names the document it was made from and lists the points a BMS uses most; the free workspace has them bound and ready.
Do the workspaces work with my device's instance number?+
Yes. Discover your device first; when a workspace reads another device instance, BACManager asks which discovered device takes its place and moves every point over.
Were the guides tested on real equipment?+
They were made from the makers' documents and tested against simulated devices built from those object lists; the pictures say so. Check each guide against your device's firmware before commissioning.
