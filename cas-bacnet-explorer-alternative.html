// being fair

When CAS BACnet Explorer is the right call

If your work is entirely BACnet — IP, MS/TP or SC — and there's no schedule that needs a real editing grid and no Modbus device on the site, CAS BACnet Explorer is free, capable, and actively maintained. It's also currently ahead on BACnet/SC, which BACManager doesn't support yet. BACManager earns its price on the messier sites: Modbus meters or drives sharing the panel, schedules that need on-site editing, or a PLC that needs BACnet data without speaking BACnet itself. The fastest way to know which camp a given site falls into is to run BACManager's free trial against it.

// questions

vs CAS BACnet Explorer — FAQ

Is CAS BACnet Explorer really free?+
Yes, per Chipkin's own product page it is free to download and use, including commercial and OEM work, with no license key required.
Does CAS BACnet Explorer support MS/TP?+
Yes — it supports BACnet/IP, MS/TP over RS-485 with a standard USB-to-RS485 adapter, and BACnet/SC (Secure Connect).
What does BACManager add that CAS BACnet Explorer doesn't have?+
A Schedule and Calendar editor with an editable weekly grid, Modbus RTU/TCP polling in the same window, and a Gateway that bridges BACnet present-values into a Modbus map a PLC can read and write.
Does BACManager support BACnet/SC?+
Not yet — only BACnet/IP and MS/TP. If a site specifically requires BACnet Secure Connect, CAS BACnet Explorer currently supports it and BACManager does not.
Is the BACManager price a subscription?+
No. BACManager is a one-time $149 license with free updates within the major version — no subscription, no per-point fees.
